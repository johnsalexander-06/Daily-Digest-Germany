Boeing lands F-15 contract worth up to $131.2 billion

The Air Force has awarded Boeing a broad indefinite-delivery contract for the F-15 line valued at up to $131.2 billion, per a Defense Department announcement — one of the largest fighter awards ever placed. The ceiling covers production, sustainment and likely foreign military sales over the life of the vehicle, effectively locking the F-15EX into the force structure well past 2040. So WhatThe high-low fighter mix is now contractually durable, which stabilizes long-range basing, manpower and maintenance-training assumptions. Check whether your unit's fleet-transition and MOS/AFSC manning plans still assume an F-15 sundown date — several do, and they are now wrong.

ISW: Ratcliffe's Moscow trip suggests Russia may be preparing a near-term act of aggression against NATO

So WhatThe warning problem has inverted: the attack profile Russia is most likely to use generates almost no classical indicators and could arrive with days, not months, of warning.
