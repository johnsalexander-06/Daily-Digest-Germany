🏛️ Tag des offenen Denkmals — Open Monument Day

Across Stuttgart and Baden-Württemberg · Sunday 13 September · Free

One day a year, historic buildings normally closed to the public open their doors with guided tours, talks and special exhibitions — everything from Roman remains and half-timbered town houses to industrial monuments and private chapels. For a family posted here, this is the single highest-value free day on the German cultural calendar, and many sites offer tours specifically aimed at children.

Insider tip: Search the national site by postcode the night before and pick two or three nearby sites — tours fill quickly and most run only once or twice. Rarity flag: many of these buildings are genuinely not accessible on any other day of the year.
