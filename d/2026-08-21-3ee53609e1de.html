A mother and son spent five years animating a film about a calf — and accidentally made $4 million

Niu Lai ("Here Comes the Cow") is an 86-minute animated dream sequence about a newborn calf, made by exactly two people: a self-taught director and his mother. There was no marketing and no trailer, and the animation is famously stiff. Its first ten days earned a grand total of about $1,000.
