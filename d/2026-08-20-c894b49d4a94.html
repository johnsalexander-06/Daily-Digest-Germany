Army selects Hanwha's wheeled K9 for self-propelled howitzer prototypes

The Army has picked Hanwha's wheeled K9 variant to build prototypes for its next mobile self-propelled howitzer, ending the latest round of a decades-long search that previously consumed the Crusader, the Non-Line-of-Sight Cannon and Extended Range Cannon Artillery. The choice is prototype-only for now, not a production award, but it sets the technical baseline the Army will evaluate against. So WhatLong-range fires modernization is finally moving from paper to metal, and the winner is an allied platform. Ukrainian forces struck the Ufa Oil Refining Hub — three separate refineries — in Bashkortostan overnight 18–19 August, roughly 1,200 kilometers from the international border. So WhatDeep strike is now degrading Russian defense-industrial feedstock, not just fuel supply — a slower but more durable effect than refinery downtime alone. Assess Russian production of rubber, polymer and composite-dependent items as a lagging indicator over the next two quarters.
